Investment Requirement

To participate in the EB-5 program, investors must make a capital investment in a qualifying U.S. business:

  • $800,000 – If the investment is in a Targeted Employment Area (TEA), including rural areas or regions with high unemployment (150% of the national average).
  • $1,050,000 – If the investment is in a non-TEA area (urban or lower-unemployment regions).

Investment funds must remain “at risk” throughout the EB-5 process to comply with USCIS regulations.

Job Creation Requirement

Each EB-5 investment must create or preserve at least 10 full-time jobs for qualifying U.S. workers within two years of the investor’s I-526E petition approval:

  • Direct Jobs – Created by the business where the investment is made (applies to developer-led EB-5 investments).
  • Indirect & Induced Jobs – Applicable only for investments through USCIS-designated regional centers.

Job creation must be documented and verified in accordance with USCIS guidelines.

What is the EB-5 program?

What is the EB-5 Program?

The United States Congress created the EB-5 Immigrant Investor Program in 1990 to stimulate the U.S. economy through job creation and capital investment by foreign investors. It works by linking US businesses looking to raise capital with foreign investors seeking a US visa, and creating American jobs. Thereby, developing a system that benefits all parties.

Through the EB-5 investment, the investor, his/her spouse, and children under the age of 21 will be granted permanent residence in the US.

Illustrative EB-5 Immigration Timeline

  1. Start

    Investor retains an immigration attorney who conducts a review of the investor’s funds and supporting documentation to confirm eligibility under the EB-5 program.

  2. Month 1

    Investor subscribes to an EB-5-qualified project, deposits the required investment amount, and pays the administrative fee. The immigration attorney then prepares and files the I-526 petition with USCIS to initiate the EB-5 visa process.

  3. Months 2-24

    USCIS reviews the I-526 Petition. Upon approval, the investor and qualifying family members receive a two-year “conditional” Green Card, which grants residency in the U.S., allowing them to live, work, and travel without restriction.

  4. Year 2+

    Approved investors and immediate family members can continue to reside, work, and travel in the U.S. with their conditional Green Card. Approximately 90 days prior to the expiration of the conditional Green Card, the attorney files the I-829 Petition to remove the conditions, confirming that the investment has met the job creation requirements.

  5. Year 3+

    If the I-829 Petition is approved, the investor and family members receive permanent residency. At this stage, the investor may also be eligible for a return of capital, and may choose to reinvest in a new project with Driftwood Capital or pursue other investment opportunities.

Actual timeline will vary based on several variables, including but not limited to the pace at which USCIS reviews filings, changes in law, regulations and guidelines, and other factors outside of the control of Driftwood and its affiliates.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the investment process in the EB-5 program?+

The investment process in the EB-5 program generally follows these steps:

  • Identify and select a business project in which to invest.
  • Make the capital investment and submit the Form I-526 application to the United States Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS).
  • If the application is approved, the investor must apply for an immigrant visa or adjust status if already in the United States.
  • Obtain a conditional permanent residence card for a period of two years.
  • File Form I-829 before conditional residency expires to remove the conditions and become a permanent resident.
How long does it take to obtain permanent residency through the EB-5 program?+
The time it takes to obtain permanent residency through the EB-5 program will vary on a case-by-case basis. However, in optimal situations with no conditional requirements, it is generally estimated to take between 24 and 60 months from filing the initial application to obtaining permanent residency.
Is it possible to include my family on my EB-5 application?+
Yes, the spouse and unmarried children under 21 years of age of the main investor can be included in the application and are also eligible to obtain a permanent resident card.
Can I live and work anywhere in the United States with a Green Card obtained through the EB-5 program?+
Yes, once you obtain a Green Card through the EB-5 program, you can live and work anywhere in the United States without restrictions. Note, however, the EB-5 application alone does not grant work authorization and a work visa may be required until the Green Card is obtained.
What happens if my business venture does not meet the employment requirements of the EB-5 program?+
If the business project you have invested in does not meet the job creation requirements, your application to remove conditions (Form I-829) may be denied and you could lose your permanent resident status.
What is a Regional Center in the context of the EB-5 program?+
A Regional Center is an economic unit designated by USCIS that promotes economic growth through EB-5 investments.
Can I invest directly without going through a Regional Center?+
Yes, direct investment is possible but requires active management and direct job creation.
What is the difference between direct and Regional Center investments?+
Direct investments require active management and direct job creation, while Regional Center investments allow for indirect job creation and typically involve less day-to-day management. Indirect jobs are held outside of the new commercial enterprise but are created as a result of the new commercial enterprise.
What is conditional permanent residency?+
It’s a two-year residency period during which the investor must meet EB-5 requirements to remove conditions.
Can I work in the U.S. while my EB-5 application is pending?+
You may need to obtain a separate work visa, as the EB-5 application alone does not grant work authorization.
