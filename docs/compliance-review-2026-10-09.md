# DealDirect pre-launch compliance review (2026-10-09)

> **This is a review aid, not legal advice.** Securities, immigration and tax counsel, the CCO and (for the DST) Metric Financial's registered principal must sign off before go-live. The proposed wording is a draft for counsel to review, not approved copy.

Reviewed: Home, Riverside Wharf Preferred Equity, Riverside Wharf QOZ teaser, Driftwood Hotel Income I DST, EB-5 (EN `/eb-5-investments/` and `/new-eb-5-page/`, ES, PT), plus the header, footer, request and EB-5 dialogs, exit interstitial and footnotes, as built on 2026-10-09. "Tracked": **Yes** = an open gap/check in `handoff/design/evidence-register/claims.js` covers it; **Partly** = the figure or wording is tracked but not the regulatory problem; **No** = new.

## 1. Top 5 risks

1. **Public general solicitation that fits no exemption.** Every offering page is public and shows specific terms, with "Start Investing", "Request Investor Details" and "Reserve Your Spot". The only gate is self-certification. That rules out 506(b) (with a Rule 152(a)(1) problem too) and is not 506(c) "reasonable steps to verify". The "not an offer" legend doesn't cure it. The QOZ teaser conditions the market before any PPM exists (H-01, H-02).
2. **EB-5 immigration-outcome and guarantee language in EN, ES and PT**, mixing direct and regional-center rules, with no risk disclosure (H-06 to H-08).
3. **The QOZ teaser is materially incomplete and partly wrong for this fund:** regime and tract designation not stated, it carries the Preferred footnotes, the hold period contradicts the 10-year exclusion, and one comparison-table row is wrong (H-04, H-05).
4. **Hypothetical (target) performance shown to a mass audience**, with "net" undefined (H-03).
5. **Track-record figures conflict and are mislabelled**, and the DST shows an undisclosed load (H-09, H-11).

Not found: no client testimonials or endorsements, and no third-party ratings of Driftwood itself (the rankings shown rate markets, cities and brands; see M-04, M-05).
