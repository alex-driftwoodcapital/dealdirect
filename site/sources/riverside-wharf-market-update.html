Miami’s Momentum: Major Investments, Growing Opportunity

Miami continues to attract significant commitments from leading developers and institutions. Recent investments highlight the scale of this activity:

  • Wynwood — $1.1 billion acquisition: Ken Griffin purchased approximately 30 acres from Moishe Mana as part of the initiative to bring Carnegie Mellon University to Miami. Griffin has also committed $2 billion to establish the new campus, adding a major educational and research institution to the city.
  • Brickell waterfront — $520 million acquisition: Oak Row Equities, OKO Group, and Mariposa Real Estate acquired a waterfront assemblage in 2025.
  • Brickell City Centre — $512 million acquisition: Simon Property Group purchased Swire Properties’ retail and parking interests in 2025.
  • Citadel headquarters site — $363 million acquisition: Citadel acquired its waterfront site in 2022, reinforcing its commitment to Miami as its global headquarters.
  • Mandarin Oriental residences — $1.7 billion in presales: On nearby Brickell Key, Swire Properties reported $1.7 billion in condominium sales before groundbreaking on The Residences at Mandarin Oriental, Miami, including two penthouses priced at approximately $50 million each.

We believe this continued investment strengthens Miami’s appeal as a destination for business, residents, and visitors—and supports the long-term opportunity for Riverside Wharf’s combination of hospitality, dining, entertainment, and events.
